I am currently exploring how to design an electromagnetic coil for wireless neural stimulation using magnetoelectric nanoparticles, with a focus on keeping tissue heating within safe limits. Using COMSOL, I iterate on different coil geometries to understand how they influence both magnetic field strength and temperature distribution. In parallel, I am also simulating core–shell piezoelectric/magnetoelectric nanoparticles to study how they can mechanically stimulate neurons under external fields. What I find most interesting is the balance between efficiency, safety, and physical constraints, especially when considering integration with imaging systems. This work is ongoing, and I am continuously refining both the coil design and the particle response through simulation.
