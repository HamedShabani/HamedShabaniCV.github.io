## Current Work



I am currently exploring how to design an electromagnetic coil for wireless neural stimulation using magnetoelectric nanoparticles, with a focus on keeping tissue heating within safe limits. Using COMSOL, I iterate on different coil geometries to understand how they influence both magnetic field strength and temperature distribution. In parallel, I am also simulating core–shell piezoelectric/magnetoelectric nanoparticles to study how they can mechanically stimulate neurons under external fields. I keep running into the same trade-off between getting strong enough fields and not overheating the tissue, which has been interesting (and sometimes frustrating) to work through. This work is ongoing, and I am continuously refining both the coil design and the particle response through simulation.
