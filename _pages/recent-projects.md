## Current Work

![Coil simulation](/images/coil-field.png)

I am currently working on coil simulations in COMSOL to see how the design can produce enough stimulation while keeping tissue heating within a safe range. In parallel, I am also simulating core–shell particles to better understand how they might mechanically stimulate neurons. This is still ongoing work, and I am learning through each iteration how geometry, heating, and stimulation response affect one another.
