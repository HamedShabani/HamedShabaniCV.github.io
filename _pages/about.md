I am an electronics engineer currently working as a researcher at FAU, where my focus is on the practical optimization of electromagnetic coils. My daily work involves using COMSOL to solve the thermal and magnetic challenges of wireless stimulation.Essentially trying to make the hardware more efficient and reliable.

I have spent time in neuroscience wetlabs, which gave me a deep respect for the complexity of biological data, but I find my greatest satisfaction in the engineering side of the lab. I’m looking to bring my experience in simulation and sensor design to a team where I can contribute to building solid, well-engineered technology.

Outside of the lab, I stay grounded by cycling, swimming, and exploring the outdoors.
